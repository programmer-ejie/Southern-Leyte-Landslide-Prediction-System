The Challenge

Landslide risk needs clearer signals

Southern Leyte communities need timely interpretation of rainfall, terrain, and hazard indicators before slope failures occur.

Manual Risk Checking

Rainfall, slope, and soil details can be hard to compare quickly when officials need a practical risk summary.

Changing Rainfall Conditions

Heavy rainfall can change susceptibility fast, so responders need scenario-based predictions and alert-ready summaries.

Scattered Hazard Data

Maps, rainfall readings, reports, and location records are easier to act on when viewed in one monitoring dashboard.
